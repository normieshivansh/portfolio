Hello!

Hey, I'm Shivansh — a B.Com (Honours) student at Amity University Lucknow by day, and a video editor with five years of practice behind me. I picked up After Effects and Premiere Pro out of curiosity and never really put them down since; somewhere along the way I started picking up a camera too, so now I edit and shoot in equal measure.

On the craft side: montage, narrative, documentary and SaaS-style brand edits in After Effects and Premiere Pro, fast-turnaround cuts in CapCut, and graphic design in Canva.

Right now I'm part of Amity University's central social media team, and I'm the Student Head of RACESPH. I've collaborated with a few agencies on brand work, and run a small crew of shoot professionals for jobs that need more hands than mine.
